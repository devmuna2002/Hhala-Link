Our Story

Built for Zimbabwe

Hlala Link started with a simple belief: everybody deserves a home. Renting in Zimbabwe is hard — no central place to search, too many middlemen, hidden fees and tenants who never know where they stand.

We built one platform where tenants can find verified properties, landlords can manage their listings, and movers can connect with customers.
